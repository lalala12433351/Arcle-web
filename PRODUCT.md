# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and marketing leads in India who need paid media (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube) to produce qualified leads, sales and measurable return — not vanity metrics.

## Product Purpose

Arcle is an AI-powered digital marketing agency. Performance marketing plans, launches and optimises paid campaigns against clear KPIs (leads, CAC, ROAS, revenue) with tracking, creative and landing pages under one team.

## Positioning

Marketing, design and development in one team so ads, SEO, social, website and app work as one growth engine. Clients keep ownership of domains, ad accounts, content and data. After setup, engagements can run month to month.
